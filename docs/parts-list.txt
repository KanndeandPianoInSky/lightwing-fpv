Lightwing 5-Inch FPV Quadcopter
Bill of Materials

Airframe
  Frame               Custom-fabricated frame. Fabrication drawings are
                      included in the hardware/drawings directory.
  Fuselage body       Cylindrical plastic container, 65 mm in diameter
                      and 100 mm in height.

Propulsion and Power
  Motors              4 x EMAX RS2205, 2300 KV.
  Motor wire sleeves  4 sleeves to secure the motor leads.
  Speed controller    LANRC 60 A four-in-one ESC. A 45 A version is an
                      option for less demanding flight profiles.
  Propellers          DALPROP T5046C, 5-inch diameter.
  Flight battery      3S or 4S, selected according to flight profile.

Flight Control and Radio
  Flight controller   Aocoda-RC F405 V2 with an MPU6500 or MPU6000 gyroscope.
  Radio transmitter   FlySky FS-i6.
  Radio receiver      FlySky FS-iA6B.

FPV Video System
  Camera              Caddx ANT.
  Video transmitter   JHEMCU RuiBet Tran-3016W.
  Antenna             Foxeer Lollipop 4, 5.8 GHz, short version with an
                      SMA connector (female center contact).
  Antenna adapter     1 x MMCX-to-SMA 90-degree right-angle adapter to
                      connect the antenna to the video transmitter.
